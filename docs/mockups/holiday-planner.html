Questions

Short answers about rooms, holidays and your account.

Your account
How do I change my status (full-time or freelancer)?

Ask one of the admins. They can change it in Admin → Users.

Admins: quinten@paradisecity.be

Why do I have to choose full-time or freelancer?
Full-time employees' holidays come in automatically from Sodify. Freelancers aren't in Sodify, so they add their own days off here. Your choice tells the planner where to look.
I forgot my password.
On the login page, click “Forgot password” and we'll email you a link.
Holidays
I'm full-time. How do I book a holiday?
Request it in Sodify as you do today. Once it's approved it shows up in the planner after the next sync (every morning, or right away when an admin clicks “Sync now”). Requests waiting for approval show with a dashed outline.
I'm a freelancer. How do I add my days off?
Open Holidays and click “Add days off”. Pick the dates, and a morning or afternoon if it's a half day. Your days appear for everyone straight away. To remove them, use “Your days off” on the right.
Who can see my holidays?
Everyone with an account sees your name on the days you're off. Nobody sees why: leave types, sick leave and balances stay in Sodify.
Are public holidays included?
Yes. Belgian public holidays are marked in the calendar and aren't counted as anyone's day off.
Meeting rooms
How do I book a meeting room?
Open Rooms, then click or drag in the calendar. You can add guests, repeat a booking, or join the waiting list when a room is taken.

Add days off

Colleagues will see your name on these days. Weekends and public holidays are skipped.
